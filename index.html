02 / How I work

A metric only counts
if it is honest.

It is easy to inflate a result without noticing. These are the three rules I follow across all three projects, and the reason my numbers are lower than they could look.

01

The test set is touched once

A 60/20/20 split. Model, hyperparameters and threshold are all chosen by looking at validation. The test set is used at the end, with the winner, and takes part in no decision.

02

Every number with its spread

A single figure from a single split says nothing. I publish cross-validation with its standard deviation: R² 0.847 ± 0.007 is more useful, and more honest, than a lucky 0.91.

03

The right metric for the problem

With 0.17% fraud, ROC-AUC looks good almost always and misleads. That is why project 03 is judged on PR-AUC, and project 02 on macro F1 rather than accuracy.
